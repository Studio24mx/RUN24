# RUN24 — External Playtest Protocol

Build: SIGNALPUNK Final Prototype 1
Target session: 12–20 minutes
Platform: Desktop Web browser, keyboard/mouse or standard gamepad.

## Tester instruction
Say only: "Play until you win or die. Think aloud if you can."
Do not explain CORE, specials, gates, moving relays or boss phases. Discoverability is part of the test.

## Observer checklist
Record:
- Did the tester understand movement without help?
- Did they understand independent aiming?
- Did they understand that combat recharges CORE?
- Which of the five specials did they discover naturally?
- Did they intentionally use down-through platforms?
- Did they understand why arena gates opened?
- Did moving relays read as usable platforms?
- First point where they lost health.
- First point where they became stuck or confused.
- Which enemy was hardest to read?
- Did CARGADOR telegraph its charge clearly?
- Did the difficulty rise naturally across the three acts?
- Did THE IDOL feel fair and learnable?
- Which visual landmark or character did they remember?
- Total run time and result from the end screen.

## Questions immediately after the run
1. In one sentence, what do you think RUN24 is about?
2. What visual element do you remember first?
3. Which of RUNNER 24, XOLO, an enemy or THE IDOL was most memorable?
4. Which special attack felt best?
5. Was any control difficult to discover?
6. Did any hit feel unreadable or unfair?
7. Where did the level feel slow or repetitive?
8. Where did the difficulty spike too much or too little?
9. Did the longer level feel satisfying or exhausting?
10. Would you immediately play another run? Why?
11. What is the one thing you would change first?

## Session metrics
The final screen reports:
- victory/failure
- run time
- enemies defeated
- registered hits
- damage taken
- specials used
- pickups collected

A JSON summary is also written to user://last_playtest.json when supported by the platform.
